Customers who prefer not to pay the whole cost at once can ask about finance,
and many banks and non-banking finance companies lend against rooftop solar,
in some cases as a home-improvement or priority-sector loan.

## What Swaraj does

The team explains the options available at the time and puts the customer in
touch with the lender. Swaraj does not lend.

## Rates and eligibility

Interest rates, tenure, down payment and eligibility are set by the lender and
change. Nobody at Swaraj quotes an EMI or an interest rate from memory — the
figures come from the lender for the customer's own case.
