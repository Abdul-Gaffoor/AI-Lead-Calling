PM Surya Ghar is the central government scheme supporting rooftop solar on
residential properties. Swaraj handles installations under the scheme and helps
customers through the paperwork.

## Who it is generally for

Residential properties, with the applicant owning the property and holding a
domestic electricity connection. The roof must have usable area. Commercial and
industrial properties are covered by other arrangements, not this scheme.

## What the process involves

Registration on the national portal, selection of a registered vendor,
installation, inspection and meter change by the distribution company, and the
subsidy paid to the customer's bank account after commissioning.

## Subsidy amounts, eligibility rules and documents

Scheme rules, subsidy slabs and the required documents change, and they are
confirmed against the current official scheme and the customer's distribution
company — never quoted from memory. The team confirms what applies to a
specific customer before anything is promised.
