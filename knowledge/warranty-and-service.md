## What is usually covered

Solar panels carry a manufacturer's performance warranty over a long term, and
a separate, shorter product warranty. Inverters carry a manufacturer's warranty
of their own. Swaraj's installation workmanship is covered separately from the
equipment.

## Confirming terms

Warranty periods and what they cover vary by manufacturer and by product, and
the terms that apply are the ones written in the customer's own quotation and
warranty documents. The team confirms them rather than quoting a period from
memory.

## After installation

Existing customers can raise a service request for low generation, an inverter
fault, wiring, monitoring, battery problems or panel cleaning. Swaraj services
existing installations whether or not it installed them.
