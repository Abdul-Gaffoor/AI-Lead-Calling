Net metering is the arrangement that lets a solar system export what it does
not use.

## The idea

A bi-directional meter records both the units drawn from the grid and the units
exported to it. Over a billing cycle the export is set against the import, and
the customer is billed on the difference.

## What has to happen

The distribution company approves the connection, the meter is changed, and the
system is inspected before it is commissioned. Swaraj handles the application
and coordinates the inspection.

## Terms

Export rates, the cap on system size relative to the sanctioned load, and how
surplus credits are settled are set by the distribution company and change over
time. The team confirms the current terms for the customer's own connection.
