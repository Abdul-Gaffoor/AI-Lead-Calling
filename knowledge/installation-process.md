## The sequence

A site survey, then a written quotation based on it, then the order. The
distribution company application and approval follow, then installation, then
inspection and the meter change, and finally commissioning — the point at which
the system starts generating and, for an on-grid system, exporting.

## The site survey

An engineer measures the usable roof area, checks shading through the day,
looks at the existing electrical connection and the earthing, and confirms how
equipment will be brought to the roof. Booking one is free and does not commit
the customer to anything.

## Timelines

How long each step takes depends on the distribution company's approval and
inspection schedule, which Swaraj does not control. The team gives a realistic
timeline once the survey is done.
