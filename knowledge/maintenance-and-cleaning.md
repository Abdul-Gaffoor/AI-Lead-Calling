## Why cleaning matters

Dust on the glass blocks light, and in this region panels collect dust quickly,
especially in the dry months and near roads or construction. Output falls
gradually, so it is easy to miss. Regular cleaning is the single cheapest thing
a customer can do for generation.

## Common causes of low generation

Dust or bird droppings on the panels. New shading from a tree or a neighbouring
structure that was not there at installation. An inverter fault or error code.
A loose or damaged connection. A faulty meter.

## What the service team needs

The system capacity, roughly when it was installed, what the customer is
seeing — an error code on the inverter display, a drop in units, or a complete
stop — and how urgent it is. A service ticket is raised from that.
