A rooftop solar system generates electricity at the house and reduces what the
household draws from the grid.

## What makes a house suitable

A roof with usable, largely unshaded area for most of the day. Ownership of the
property, or the owner's permission. An existing electricity connection in the
household's name. Shading from a neighbouring building, a water tank or a tree
matters more than most customers expect, which is why the engineer looks at the
roof rather than working from a photograph.

## What the engineer needs to know

Recent electricity bills, ideally the monthly units rather than only the
rupee amount, because the units are what the system is sized against. The type
of roof — flat concrete, sloped, or sheet — and the approximate usable area.
Whether the connection is single-phase or three-phase. Whether the household
wants battery backup.

## Sizing and cost

System size, generation, savings and payback are calculated by Swaraj's
engineering team from the site survey and the customer's actual consumption.
Those figures are never estimated over the phone.
