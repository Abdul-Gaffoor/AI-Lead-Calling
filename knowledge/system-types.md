## On-grid

The system works alongside the distribution company's supply. Generation is
used in the building first and any excess is exported to the grid. There is no
battery, so an on-grid system does not run during a power cut — the inverter
shuts down for the safety of anyone working on the line. This is the usual
choice where supply is reliable.

## Hybrid

An on-grid system with battery storage added, so selected circuits keep running
during a power cut and stored energy can be used in the evening. It costs more
than on-grid because of the battery.

## Off-grid

No connection to the distribution company. Everything runs from panels and
batteries, which means the battery bank has to carry the whole load. Used where
there is no reliable supply at all.

## Choosing

The choice follows how often supply fails at the site, what must keep running
during a failure, and the customer's budget. The engineer discusses it during
the survey.
