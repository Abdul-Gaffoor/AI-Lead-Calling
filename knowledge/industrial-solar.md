Larger installations for manufacturing and processing sites, typically on an
HT connection.

## What the engineering team needs

Whether the supply is HT or LT, the transformer capacity and the maximum
demand. Operating hours and shift pattern, since a plant running two or three
shifts uses power when solar does not generate. Roof type and area, plus any
open land. Existing diesel generator use. Any solar already installed, and
whether the site wants to expand it.

## Handling

Industrial enquiries are routed to a senior executive or engineer rather than
being qualified and queued like a household enquiry. The technical survey comes
before any discussion of capacity or cost.
