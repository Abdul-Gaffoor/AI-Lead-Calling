Solar for businesses — offices, retail, hospitals, educational institutions,
warehouses — on rooftops or on open land at the site.

## What shapes a commercial proposal

The sanctioned load and contract demand on the connection, the monthly
consumption profile, and whether the business runs mainly during daylight
hours, since daytime consumption is matched directly by generation. Whether the
premises are owned or leased, and the remaining term of the lease. Available
roof or open land area.

## Who needs to be involved

Commercial decisions usually involve more than one person. Knowing early who
signs off, and what the approval process looks like, avoids a survey and
quotation going to someone who cannot act on it.

## Figures

Capacity, cost and returns follow the technical survey. A commercial enquiry
that needs numbers goes to a Swaraj executive, not to an estimate on the phone.
