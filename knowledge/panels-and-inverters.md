## Panels

Swaraj installs panels from established manufacturers. The relevant differences
between panel types are efficiency — how much power comes from a given area,
which matters when the roof is small — and the performance warranty.

## Inverters

The inverter converts panel output into the alternating current the building
uses, and in an on-grid system it is what synchronises with the grid and shuts
down safely during an outage. Inverter capacity is matched to the array during
design.

## Which product

The specific make and model quoted for a customer comes from the engineering
team after the survey, since it depends on the roof, the connection and what is
currently available. It is not decided on a phone call.
