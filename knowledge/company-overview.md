Swaraj Solar designs, installs and maintains solar power systems for customers
across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh. Work covers homes, businesses, industrial
sites and agricultural land.

## What Swaraj does

Rooftop solar for homes, including installations under the PM Surya Ghar
scheme. Commercial rooftop and ground-mounted systems for businesses.
Larger industrial installations. Solar pumping and ground-mounted systems for
agriculture. Service, maintenance and panel cleaning for existing systems,
whether or not Swaraj installed them.

## How an enquiry usually proceeds

A customer enquires, the team understands the property and electricity use, an
engineer visits for a site survey, and a written quotation follows the survey.
Nothing is quoted before someone has seen the site, because roof area, shading
and the existing electrical connection all change what is possible.

## Languages

The team works in Telugu and English, and customers are welcome to switch
between them.
