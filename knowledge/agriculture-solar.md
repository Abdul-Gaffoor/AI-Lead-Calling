Solar pumping and ground-mounted systems for farms.

## What matters on a farm

Ownership of the land and its approximate area. Whether there is an
agricultural electricity connection, and how reliable supply is. The pump
rating in horsepower and the water source — borewell, open well or canal.
Whether the requirement is a solar pump, a ground-mounted generating system, or
both.

## Location

Village, mandal and district, because access and the distribution company
differ across the region and both affect what can be installed.

## Schemes

Where a state or central scheme applies to agricultural solar, the current
terms are confirmed by the team against the scheme's own rules.
