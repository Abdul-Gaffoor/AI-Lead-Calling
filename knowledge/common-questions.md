## Will solar run my house during a power cut?

Only if there is a battery. A standard on-grid system shuts down during an
outage, deliberately, so that it cannot energise a line someone is working on.
A hybrid system with a battery keeps selected circuits running.

## How much will it cost, and how much will I save?

That depends on the roof, the consumption and the system chosen, and it is
calculated by Swaraj's engineering team — from the site survey and the
customer's actual bills — rather than estimated on a call. The survey is free.

## How long do panels last?

Panels are long-life equipment and carry a manufacturer's performance warranty
over many years. The exact term is in the customer's own warranty document.

## Does the roof need to be empty?

No, but the system needs unshaded usable area. A water tank, a stairwell or a
neighbouring wall can shade part of the roof at some times of day, which the
engineer checks during the survey.

## Do I have to clean the panels myself?

No. Swaraj offers cleaning and maintenance, and existing customers can raise a
service request.

## Is my area covered?

Swaraj works across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh. The team confirms coverage
for a specific village or mandal.
